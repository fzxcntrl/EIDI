Beautiful Sunnahs of Eid

🚿

Performing Ghusl

Purify yourself before the Eid prayer.

👔

Wearing Best Clothes

Adorn yourself with your best, clean garments.

🌴

Eating Dates

Eat an odd number of dates before leaving for Salat al-Eid (Eid ul-Fitr).

🚶

Taking Different Routes

Go to the prayer using one route and return via another.

📿

Reciting Takbeer

Recite Takbeer continuously on the way to the prayer ground.

🤝

Greeting One Another

Say "Taqabbalallahu Minna wa Minkum" (May Allah accept [good deeds] from us and from you).

🕌

Walking to Prayer

It is recommended to walk to the Eid prayer if possible.
